Who We Are

A Decade of Trust, Innovation & Execution

Founded in Turkey in 2012, Cortexa Electronics began by delivering comprehensive technology solutions to international and local organizations, small businesses, and commercial enterprises across Syria.

In 2025, we expanded into direct field operations inside Syria, bringing our expertise even closer to the institutions and communities we serve.

Over more than a decade, we have built an extensive portfolio and a trusted network of clients — backed by a strong track record in executing large-scale projects. We don't just provide technology services; we build the infrastructure of the future.

With over 13 years of experience at the heart of the technology market, Cortexa Electronics stands out as a leading strategic partner in IT, telecom, security, protection, and defense solutions.
